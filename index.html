Important Note for Applicants

While this guide provides verified structural and modular breakdowns from official university sources, curricula are subject to minor annual updates. For the most granular, semester-by-semester ECTS breakdowns, prerequisite chains, and current course schedules, always download the latest official "Modulhandbuch" (Module Handbook) or "Studienverlaufsplan" (Study Plan) PDF directly from the respective program's "Downloads" or "Regulations" webpage. These documents are the legally binding references for your studies.
